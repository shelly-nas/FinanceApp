Behaviour

· Nothing ticked means all. Ticking narrows.

· "only" on hover selects just that item, the quickest way to look at one account or category.

· The group heading ticks or clears the whole group (e.g. all savings accounts).

· Changes apply at once; there is no "Apply" button.

· State lives in the URL (?period=12m&to=2026-10&accounts=…&categories=…), so reload, back and a shared link all keep it.

· Remembered between visits: the accounts. The period starts at the current month again, categories at all.
